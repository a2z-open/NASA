# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite and TypeScript browser surface backed by FastAPI.

## Users

Hackathon judges and visitors who want to compare archived observations in the same region of the sky.

## Product Purpose

Play chronological real SPHEREx cutouts and make source, date, bandpass, and data limitations visible at each frame.

## Operating Context

Visitors start with Barnard’s Star, switch to another curated target, or search ICRS coordinates. Desktop and phone browsers must support both manual stepping and playback.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The first build slice plays real data from NASA/IPAC IRSA. The reserved Barnard’s Star visit stays out of playback. Wavelength labels distinguish verified and approximate data. The motion verdict requires its own measured and preregistered result.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native controls, visible keyboard focus, text alternatives for images, and explicit play or pause.
